## Business problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market in Nairobi. For two weeks she has recorded her daily sales and a rough stock list, and she wants to know how the business is doing, whether it is growing, and what she should restock and focus on. Using only core Python (no pandas), this project calculates sales totals and averages, week-on-week growth, strong and weak days, weekday versus weekend performance, low-stock items and product margins, and ends with specific recommendations.

## Top three findings

1. **Weekends sell 82.5% more than weekdays.** Weekend days average KES 4,900 against KES 2,685 on weekdays, and all four Strong days were weekends.
2. **Sales grew 10.66% week on week.** Week 1 totalled KES 22,050 and week 2 totalled KES 24,400, on a two-week total of KES 46,450.
3. **Two products are below their reorder level:** Tomatoes (18 kg against 25 kg) and Sukuma (9 kg against 15 kg). Avocado has the best margin at 42.9%.
